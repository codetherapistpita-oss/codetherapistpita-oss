The curator supplied three movie images that were enhanced in this ChatGPT session. The images have been resized to **compact landscape JPGs** while keeping the main people, dog, and identifiable movie composition visible. They are available to the user as a ZIP download in the accompanying ChatGPT answer, but **binary JPGs are not yet in the GitHub repository**, because the available GitHub editing action supports text/SVG rather than uploading local binary images.

**After upload:** replace each relevant original SVG banner in `README.md` and the corresponding story with `./assets/<filename>.jpg` and use a Markdown image with 560–680px width. Keep images readable on mobile. Do not describe an image as a genuine, unmodified studio still if it is an AI-enhanced recreation.

### Rights and attribution

These pictures originate from movie marketing/stills and user-provided screenshots with enhanced revisions. The films are owned by their respective rights holders; owning a screenshot or enhancing it does **not** establish permission for unrestricted public reuse. Use attributed stills only where authorized or a relevant legal exception applies, and provide source/studio credit. The original abstract SVG title cards can remain online independently.
