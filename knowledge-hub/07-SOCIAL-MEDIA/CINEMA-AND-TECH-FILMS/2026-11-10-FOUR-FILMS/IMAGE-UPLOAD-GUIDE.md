# Movie banner assets and publishing standard

[← Four films](./README.md)

All four cover banners are committed in assets/ and used by the collection and individual reports. Each PNG is **1080 × 450 pixels**, displayed at **720px width** with its natural aspect ratio preserved.

| Movie | Published banner |
|---|---|
| The Town | 01-town-photo-banner.png |
| Heart of the Beast | 02-heart-photo-banner.png |
| WarGames | 03-wargames-photo-banner.png |
| Sneakers | 04-sneakers-photo-banner.png |

Every future movie feature must use a clear sourced film image as its landscape background, readable title and brief thematic text in front, and the established Code Therapist cinema badge, dark readability gradient and gold accents. Preserve faces and image detail.

## Sources and adaptations

- **WarGames:** MGM/UA film still reproduced by [New Atlas](https://newatlas.com/history-hollywood-hacking-1980s/45482/).
- **Sneakers:** official high-resolution poster from [Universal Pictures At Home](https://www.universalpicturesathome.com/movies/sneakers).
- Both new banners are AI-assisted editorial adaptations of the sourced artwork, with rearranged composition and foreground typography. They are not unmodified production stills. Film imagery belongs to the respective rights holders.

## Verification before publishing

Decode the entire final image to catch incomplete files. Check exact dimensions, title spelling, readable text, image paths and the live GitHub render. Confirm each banner is fully visible.
