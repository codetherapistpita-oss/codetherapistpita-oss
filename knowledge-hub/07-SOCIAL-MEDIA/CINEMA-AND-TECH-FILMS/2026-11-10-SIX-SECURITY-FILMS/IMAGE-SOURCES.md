## Design process

Built-in image generation adapted each sourced image into a landscape editorial composition, preserving recognisable film subjects while rearranging the artwork. The existing The Town banner supplied the style reference: dark navy readability gradient, gold Code Therapist badge, white foreground title, short gold category line, brief tagline and gold rule. Original poster credits and typography were replaced. These are AI-assisted editorial adaptations, not unmodified production photographs. Film artwork remains the property of the respective rights holders.

The generation brief required 1080 × 450 proportions, readable text within safe margins, clear faces, no generic abstract placeholder and no actors imported from the style reference. Final exports were resized to the exact dimensions and fully decoded for validation.
