## Stage-gate acceptance

| Gate | Indicative checkpoint | Evidence sought |
|---|---|---|
| Requirements baseline | By end of Month 3 | Research, SRS, architecture, UX blueprint approval |
| Core pilot application | By end of Month 6 | Demonstration of major authenticated property journeys |
| Controlled pilot | Months 7–8 | Approved test users, findings, corrective action decisions |
| Production rehearsal | By end of Month 11 | Security, capacity, recovery and migration evidence |
| Handover | End Month 12 | Documentation, training, acceptance and operating ownership |

## Stage roles and responsibilities

The original private delivery planning describes the workstreams and acceptance gates but **does not establish individual named owners for every project task** in this public edition. Assign owners only when confirmed.

## Schedule risks

Supplier or provider onboarding, access approvals, pilot findings, third-party integrations, scope changes and missing review decisions may affect delivery. A target date does not equal automatic acceptance.
