## Books and long-form references

| Resource | Level | Why it matters | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fundamentals of Computer Graphics, 5th ed.** — Marschner & Shirley | Intermediate | Geometry, rendering, sampling, textures and core graphics theory | https://www.routledge.com/Fundamentals-of-Computer-Graphics/Shirley-Marschner/p/book/9780367505585 |
| **Real-Time Rendering, 4th ed.** — Akenine-Möller et al. | Intermediate/advanced | Real-time pipelines, GPUs, shading, lighting and interactive rendering | https://www.realtimerendering.com/ |
| **Physically Based Rendering, 4th ed.** — Pharr, Jakob & Humphreys | Advanced | Photorealistic rendering theory and implementation; official full online edition available | https://pbrt.org/ |
