# History of Computing — Chronological Learning Path

A staged reading plan for a future sourced series. **These are planned chapters, not completed articles.**

| Part | Period and theme | Planned coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Ancient and early calculation | Abacus and mathematical calculation tools |
| 02 | Mechanical computation | Calculators, engines, punch-card concepts |
| 03 | Electromechanical era | Relays, tabulating and early programmable systems |
| 04 | Electronic computing | Vacuum tubes, early electronic computers and debates over firsts |
| 05 | Transistor and integrated-circuit era | Miniaturization and computer generations |
| 06 | Personal computer era | Microprocessors, home computers and PC ecosystems |
| 07 | Networks and the internet | Packet switching, research networks and the Web |
| 08 | Modern computing | Mobile, cloud, GPUs and large-scale platforms |
| 09 | AI and emerging computing | Machine learning, advanced chips and quantum research |

Each chapter should include **chronology, key figures, important devices, visuals, primary sources and what changed technically**. Historical “first” claims require careful qualification.
