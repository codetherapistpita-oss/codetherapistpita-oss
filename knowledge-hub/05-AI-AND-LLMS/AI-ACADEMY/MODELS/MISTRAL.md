# 🤖 Mistral — AI Family Study Profile

**Developer:** Mistral AI · **Origins:** 2023 · **Profile reviewed:** 9 November 2026

[← 15-model catalog](../MODEL-CATALOG.md) · [← AI Academy](../README.md)

> **Important distinction:** Mix of open-weight and commercial models, plus chat applications. A consumer chatbot, underlying model, API and open-weight distribution are not necessarily the same product. Avoid comparing model families by marketing claims alone.

## 1. Where this AI came from

Mistral AI launched model releases in 2023 and expanded into mixture-of-experts and enterprise-focused AI. [Official background](https://mistral.ai/news).

## 2. What it does

**Typical applications:** Multilingual assistance, coding, enterprise APIs and self-hosted alternatives.

**Current research note:** Verify each model's license: not every Mistral product is an open-weight model. This page summarizes a model **family**; it does not claim a single version is universally best or list unsupported prices and benchmark rankings.

## 3. How a learner can use or investigate it

1. Open the [official entry point](https://chat.mistral.ai/) and check account requirements and access in your region.
2. Start with a **non-confidential** task, using a clear objective and an example.
3. Request a structured answer, assumptions, source links where available and uncertainty.
4. Check important claims against original sources; run code before trusting it.
5. Compare the result on the same task with another model family in the catalog.

**Lab exercise:** Compare official documentation for an open-weight Mistral model and a hosted model: list three deployment tradeoffs.

## 4. Technical questions

- Is the selected version proprietary, open-weight, or under a specific custom license?
- Is the model served online or deployable locally? What hardware and storage are required?
- Which modalities and tools does the **specific version** accept?
- What data retention, training-use, privacy and organizational settings apply?
- How are answers evaluated for factual accuracy, bias, reliability, latency and cost?
