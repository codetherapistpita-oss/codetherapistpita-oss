## Lecture

Compare hosted inference, local inference and fine-tuning; estimate resource needs from documented model cards and plan rollback and monitoring. Separate the stated objective, the inputs, an observable result and what would count as failure. A confident model response or an attractive user interface alone does not establish quality.
