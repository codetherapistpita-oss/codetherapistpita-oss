## Lecture

Use an approved public article to develop a note, quiz and source-checked summary. Compare the original article with AI's output and correct errors. Separate the stated objective, the inputs, an observable result and what would count as failure. A confident model response or an attractive user interface alone does not establish quality.

## Today's assessment

1. State the practical objective in one sentence.
2. Complete the exercise using **public or synthetic information**.
3. Record one success and one limitation, supported by evidence.
4. Identify a privacy or reliability concern and its mitigation.

**Assessment: 10 marks** (concept 3, evidence 3, safety 2, reflection 2).
